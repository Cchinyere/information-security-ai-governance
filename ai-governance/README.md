# AI Governance Toolkit

## Overview

This section contains practical AI governance resources designed to support responsible, secure, and controlled AI adoption.

The resources are intended for GRC professionals, cyber security teams, compliance officers, internal auditors, risk managers, data protection teams, and organisations implementing enterprise AI systems.

The toolkit focuses on practical governance activities such as AI system registration, risk assessment, policy development, vendor due diligence, control assurance, and security oversight.

## How to Use This Toolkit

Use this toolkit to support:

1. **AI Inventory Management**  
   Maintain visibility of AI systems, owners, use cases, data categories, risk levels, and review status.

2. **AI Governance Policy Development**  
   Define rules for acceptable AI use, approval, accountability, human oversight, monitoring, and periodic review.

3. **AI Vendor and Third-Party Risk Assessment**  
   Assess external AI providers, embedded AI tools, SaaS products, APIs, and AI-enabled supplier services.

4. **AI Security Control Assurance**  
   Review whether AI systems are governed, secured, monitored, and evidenced appropriately.

5. **Internal Audit and Compliance Reviews**  
   Support audit scoping, evidence requests, control testing, and governance reporting.
