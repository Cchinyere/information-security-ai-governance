# AI Vendor Due Diligence Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist supports the assessment of third-party AI vendors, AI-enabled SaaS tools, embedded AI services, APIs, and externally hosted models.

It is designed for GRC, cyber security, compliance, procurement, legal, privacy, and internal audit teams involved in AI supplier assurance.

## Due Diligence Checklist

| Area | Question | Response | Evidence Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Governance | Has the vendor provided clear information about the AI system’s purpose and limitations? | Yes / No / Partial | Product documentation, model card, service description |
| Security | Does the vendor have recognised security certification or assurance reports? | Yes / No / Partial | ISO 27001 certificate, SOC 2 report, penetration test summary |
| Data Protection | Has the vendor explained how customer data is processed, stored, retained, and deleted? | Yes / No / Partial | DPA, privacy notice, data flow diagram |
| Data Usage | Does the vendor use customer data to train or improve models? | Yes / No / Partial | Contract terms, data processing terms |
| Access Control | Are role-based access control and MFA available? | Yes / No / Partial | Access control documentation, configuration evidence |
| Logging and Monitoring | Are user activity logs and audit trails available? | Yes / No / Partial | Audit log sample, monitoring documentation |
| Incident Response | Does the vendor have a defined incident notification process? | Yes / No / Partial | Incident response policy, SLA, contract clause |
| Model Transparency | Has the vendor explained model limitations, accuracy, and known risks? | Yes / No / Partial | Model documentation, validation report |
| Human Oversight | Does the product allow human review, override, or approval workflows? | Yes / No / Partial | Workflow documentation, configuration guide |
| Sub-processors | Has the vendor disclosed all relevant sub-processors? | Yes / No / Partial | Sub-processor list |
| Data Residency | Is the data location known and acceptable? | Yes / No / Partial | Hosting location, regional processing terms |
| Regulatory Support | Can the vendor support regulatory, audit, or compliance evidence requests? | Yes / No / Partial | Assurance pack, audit rights clause |
| Business Continuity | Does the vendor have continuity and resilience arrangements? | Yes / No / Partial | BCP/DR documentation, uptime SLA |
| Exit Management | Is there a defined exit plan and data return/deletion process? | Yes / No / Partial | Exit plan, contract termination clause |

## Risk Rating

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| Low | Vendor risk is limited and appropriate controls are in place. |
| Medium | Some control gaps or dependencies exist but can be managed with remediation or compensating controls. |
| High | Material risks exist and require senior review, remediation, contractual controls, or risk acceptance before approval. |

---

## Approval Decision

| Decision | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Approved | Vendor meets security, privacy, governance, and operational requirements. |
| Approved with Conditions | Vendor may be used if specific remediation actions or restrictions are applied. |
| Further Review Required | More information or assurance evidence is needed before approval. |
| Not Approved | Vendor presents unacceptable risk or fails to meet minimum requirements. |

## Practitioner Notes

AI vendor due diligence should be refreshed when:

- The vendor changes its AI model or service.
- New data categories are introduced.
- The service becomes business critical.
- A security, privacy, or operational incident occurs.
- Regulatory expectations change.
- The contract is renewed or materially changed.
