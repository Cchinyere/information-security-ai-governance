# AI Governance Policy Template

## Purpose

This policy template provides a practical structure for governing the responsible, secure, and controlled use of artificial intelligence within an organisation.

It is designed for organisations that need to manage AI risks across security, compliance, privacy, ethics, operational resilience, third-party risk, and internal assurance.

---

## Policy Objectives

This policy aims to ensure that AI systems are:

- Used for clearly defined and approved purposes.
- Governed with clear accountability and ownership.
- Assessed for security, privacy, legal, compliance, and operational risks.
- Subject to appropriate human oversight.
- Monitored throughout their lifecycle.
- Aligned with responsible AI principles and regulatory expectations.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- Internally developed AI systems.
- Third-party AI tools and platforms.
- AI-enabled SaaS products.
- Generative AI tools.
- Predictive models and automated decision support tools.
- AI systems used in customer, employee, operational, compliance, or security processes.

---

## Governance Requirements

| Requirement | Description |
|---|---|
| AI System Registration | All AI systems must be recorded in the AI system register before use. |
| Defined Ownership | Each AI system must have a named business owner, technical owner, and risk owner. |
| Risk Assessment | AI systems must be assessed for security, privacy, legal, compliance, operational, and ethical risks. |
| Data Governance | Data used by AI systems must be classified, approved, and protected. |
| Human Oversight | AI systems that influence material decisions must include appropriate human review. |
| Vendor Review | Third-party AI providers must be assessed before approval. |
| Monitoring | AI systems must be monitored for misuse, drift, performance issues, and control failures. |
| Incident Response | AI-related incidents must be reported, investigated, and remediated. |
| Periodic Review | AI systems must be reviewed at defined intervals based on risk level. |

---

## Acceptable Use

Users may use approved AI systems only for authorised business purposes.

Users must not:

- Input sensitive, confidential, or regulated data into unapproved AI tools.
- Use AI outputs as final decisions without required human review.
- Use AI to bypass security, compliance, legal, HR, or risk controls.
- Use AI-generated content without checking accuracy and appropriateness.
- Deploy AI systems without governance review and approval.

---

## Risk Classification

AI systems should be classified as:

| Risk Level | Description |
|---|---|
| Low | Limited business impact, no sensitive data, and no material decision influence. |
| Medium | Uses internal or personal data, supports business decisions, or involves third-party dependency. |
| High | Uses sensitive or regulated data, affects individuals or customers, supports critical operations, or has regulatory relevance. |

---

## Approval Requirements

| Risk Level | Approval Requirement |
|---|---|
| Low | Business owner approval and registration. |
| Medium | Business owner, risk owner, security/privacy review, and governance approval. |
| High | Formal governance committee review, risk owner sign-off, security/privacy assessment, and documented control evidence. |

---

## Monitoring and Review

AI systems should be reviewed:

- Before deployment.
- After material changes.
- After incidents or complaints.
- When new data sources are introduced.
- When vendors or processors change.
- At least annually, or more frequently for high-risk AI systems.

---

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Business Owner | Owns the AI use case and ensures it remains appropriate and controlled. |
| Technical Owner | Manages technical configuration, implementation, and operational support. |
| Risk Owner | Ensures AI-related risks are identified, assessed, treated, and monitored. |
| Data Protection / Privacy Team | Reviews privacy, personal data, DPIA, and lawful basis requirements. |
| Cyber Security Team | Reviews security controls, access, monitoring, and incident response. |
| Compliance Team | Reviews regulatory and policy alignment. |
| Internal Audit | Provides independent assurance over AI governance and control effectiveness. |

---

## Policy Review

This policy should be reviewed at least annually or following:

- Significant regulatory change.
- Material AI incident.
- Change in organisational AI strategy.
- Introduction of high-risk AI systems.
- Changes to security, privacy, or operational resilience requirements.
