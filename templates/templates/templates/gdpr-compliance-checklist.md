# GDPR Compliance Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist supports a structured review of data protection readiness under the UK GDPR and general GDPR-style privacy governance expectations.

It is intended for portfolio, internal assessment, privacy governance, and compliance planning purposes. It is not legal advice. Organisations should seek legal or data protection specialist advice for complex or high-risk processing.

---

## How to Use This Checklist

Suggested status values:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not Started | No documented activity or evidence exists. |
| In Progress | Some implementation exists, but it is incomplete. |
| Implemented | The requirement is documented, assigned, and operating. |
| Needs Improvement | The control exists but requires strengthening. |
| Not Applicable | The item does not apply and justification is documented. |
