# ISO/IEC 42001 AI Management Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist supports a structured review of an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) aligned with ISO/IEC 42001 management system expectations and practical responsible AI governance practices.

It is designed for portfolio, internal readiness, gap assessment, and governance planning purposes. It does not reproduce the ISO/IEC 42001 standard and should not be treated as certification advice.

---

## How to Use This Checklist

Use the checklist to assess whether an organisation has defined, implemented, monitored, and improved governance arrangements for AI systems.

Suggested status values:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not Started | No documented activity or evidence exists. |
| In Progress | Some activity exists, but it is incomplete or not consistently applied. |
| Implemented | The requirement is documented, assigned, and operating. |
| Needs Improvement | The control exists but requires strengthening, review, or better evidence. |
| Not Applicable | The item does not apply based on scope and documented justification. |
