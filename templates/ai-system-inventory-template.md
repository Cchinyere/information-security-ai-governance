# AI System Inventory Template

## Purpose

This template supports the identification, documentation, and governance of AI systems used within an organisation.

An AI system inventory helps organisations maintain visibility of AI use cases, assess associated risks, assign ownership, and support compliance with AI governance expectations, including ISO/IEC 42001 and responsible AI principles.
