## 2. Purpose

This template supports structured assessment of AI systems and AI use cases in line with AI governance and AI management system principles. It is designed to help identify risks, determine appropriate controls, support responsible AI decision-making, and prepare governance evidence.

This template can support ISO/IEC 42001-aligned activities but does not replace the need for a licensed copy of the standard, organisation-specific policy requirements, or specialist legal and regulatory review.

| Governance Approval | Business owner approval | Business and risk approval | Governance committee approval |
| Risk Assessment | Basic use case assessment | Formal AI risk assessment | Enhanced AI risk and impact assessment |

| Documentation | Basic documentation | Full use case documentation | Full governance pack and evidence trail |
| Supplier Review | Where applicable | Required for third-party systems | Enhanced supplier assurance |

| Security Review | Basic review | Security review required | Threat modelling and enhanced review |

## 10. Assumptions and Limitations

- This assessment is based on available information at the time of review.
- The risk classification should be revisited when the AI use case, data, model, supplier, operating environment, or regulatory context changes.
- Legal, privacy, employment, equality, consumer protection, sector-specific, and regulatory questions should be referred to appropriate specialists.
